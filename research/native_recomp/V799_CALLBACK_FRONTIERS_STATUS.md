# V799: callback continuation census

The original callback has 13 call sites (11 direct, two indirect). The V787 census found repeated 39-word helper candidates and three SITransfer calls. V799 assigns stable per-target groups, distinguishes hypothetical call-return continuations from observed execution, reports a mask of the first next call boundaries on each continuation, and authenticates sized direct child roots with DOL/ELF equality. It emits only bounded ordinals, counts and known-root classifications. This separates actual shared roots from coincidentally equal sizes, and narrows the next native dependency once the callback is reached. Conditional linked and noncanonical indirect forms are refused rather than silently treated as ordinary branches. Private census and synthetic tests pending; no execution claim.
